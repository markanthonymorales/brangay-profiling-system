The second milestone expands the barangay profiling system from a reporting platform into a more advanced planning and operational tool for the City Government of Davao by introducing user account management, department-based access control, approval workflows, and real-time dashboards for city officials, district coordinators, and barangay staff. The system will support scheduled data updates, automated validation of submitted information, historical tracking of changes, and side-by-side comparisons between barangays and districts across multiple years to identify growth, decline, and emerging risks. It will include map-based visualizations for crime hotspots, traffic congestion, waste accumulation, population density, business activity, and infrastructure coverage, allowing officials to quickly identify problem areas and prioritize responses. Additional forecasting features will project future needs for housing, utilities, food supply, healthcare, schools, transportation, and public safety based on current trends and historical records. The platform will also generate automated recommendations for budget allocation, emergency response planning, social services, business development, and crime prevention initiatives for each barangay. To improve reliability, the system will include automated backups, detailed activity logs, notification alerts for missing or unusual data, retry handling for failed submissions, and audit records to track who created, reviewed, approved, or changed information. This milestone ensures that the city government can move beyond static reporting and use the platform as a dependable decision-support system for long-term planning, daily operations, and faster response to community needs.
