The goal of this system is to provide the City Government of Davao with a comprehensive and reliable profiling tool for all 182 barangays, enabling informed decision-making and efficient resource management. The system collects and organizes detailed information for each barangay and district, including population data, registered and non-registered residents, foreign residents, renters, squatters, income levels, business activity, sources of food, utilities like water and power, land types, waste management, government agency locations, and religious demographics, while also mapping their precise locations. It analyzes this data to identify high-risk areas for crime and traffic, monitors current crime rates, and applies predictive analytics to forecast future trends and generate actionable plans for each barangay. Reports and summaries are automatically produced to provide totals and insights at both barangay and district levels, with built-in safeguards to ensure accuracy, prevent data loss, and maintain reliability even under repeated or high-volume use. Through continuous logging, monitoring, and automated updates, the system guarantees that city officials can access timely, consistent, and actionable information to guide public safety, infrastructure planning, and community services.
