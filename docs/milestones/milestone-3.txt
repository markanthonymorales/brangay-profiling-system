The third milestone transforms the barangay profiling system into a fully integrated city intelligence platform by connecting data from multiple government offices, public safety units, health services, social welfare offices, business permit records, disaster response teams, and utility providers into one centralized environment. The system will support live data synchronization across departments, allowing updates made by one office to immediately appear in relevant reports, maps, and dashboards used by other agencies. Advanced forecasting will expand to include disaster preparedness, flood-prone areas, fire risks, disease outbreaks, unemployment trends, migration patterns, school capacity, hospital demand, and infrastructure strain. The platform will introduce scenario planning tools that allow city officials to simulate the impact of population growth, new businesses, road projects, crime spikes, natural disasters, or utility shortages before making policy decisions. Executive dashboards will provide city leaders with real-time performance indicators, district rankings, service gaps, and priority alerts, while automated notifications will warn agencies about rising risks, overdue updates, incomplete records, or sudden changes in key indicators. The system will also support document management, digital approvals, photo attachments, and field data collection through mobile devices so barangay personnel can upload information directly from the field. Strong security controls, encrypted records, role-based access, detailed audit trails, automatic backups, failover protection, and continuous monitoring will ensure the platform remains secure, accurate, and available even during emergencies or periods of high usage, allowing the City Government of Davao to operate with faster coordination, better planning, and more data-driven decision-making.
